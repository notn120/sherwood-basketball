Doors open

Full court. Five on five.

  • Four quarters of 1:00. Teams switch ends at halftime, and a tie goes to overtime.
  • Move with W A S D. Hold Shift to sprint. Press Q to juke: a quick crossover that leaves nearby defenders stumbling.
  • Hold Space to charge a shot and release in the green zone. X passes on offense. On defense it lunges at the ball handler for a steal, and a steal starts a fast break. Press R twice to restart.
  • Prefer the classic? The 60-second shootout puts you on nine spots with no defenders.
